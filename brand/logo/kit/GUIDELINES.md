# OpenPiso WiFi — Logo guidelines

## 1. The logo
- **Idea:** one letter holds the whole name. The **p**'s bowl is an **O**, the coin. The dot at the centre of the coin sends out the WiFi. Drop a coin, get signal.
- **Colour rule:** the greens are shared with the OpenPiso client. Forest is the letter. The coin and its signal (dot, waves, and "wifi" in the wordmark) are always the lighter green: **Mint** on Forest/dark backgrounds, **Leaf** on white.
- **Versions:**

| Version | File | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Horizontal (primary) | `lockups/lockup-horizontal-color.svg` | Headers, README, portal, docs |
| Stacked | `lockups/lockup-stacked-color.svg` | Square-ish spaces, stickers, splash screens |
| Symbol | `symbol/openpiso-symbol-*.svg` (from `masters/symbol-color.svg`) | Avatars, social, anywhere the name is nearby |
| Small cut | `masters/symbol-small-*.svg` | **Only** at 32 px and below (favicon, tray icons). Heavier strokes, one wave |
| Wordmark | `masters/wordmark-*.svg` | When the symbol is already on screen |
| App icon | `symbol/openpiso-symbol-app-icon.svg`, `icon-*.png`, `apple-touch-icon.png` | Home-screen and PWA icons |

Each comes in `color` (Forest + Leaf, for light backgrounds), `reversed` (white + Mint, for Forest backgrounds), `black`, and `white` (one-colour).

## 2. Clear space
Keep at least **1 × the O bowl's width** (the coin) clear around the symbol. Around the lockups, keep **½ × the coin**. Measure it on the logo at its current size; it scales with the logo.

## 3. Minimum size
| Version | Screen | Print |
|---|---|---|
| Horizontal | 120 px wide | 30 mm wide |
| Stacked | 72 px wide | 20 mm wide |
| Symbol (full) | 40 px tall | 10 mm |
| Symbol (small cut) | 16 px | 5 mm |

## 4. Colour
| Name | HEX | RGB | CMYK (approx.) | Pantone (nearest) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Forest | `#123C1C` | 18 60 28 | 85 35 90 65 | 3435 C |
| Mint | `#71DA88` | 113 218 136 | 50 0 60 0 | 353 C |
| Leaf | `#2E9E4F` | 46 158 79 | 80 5 90 0 | 7740 C |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | 255 255 255 | 0 0 0 0 | — |

Forest and Mint match the existing OpenPiso client (`openpiso/apps/client/public`: Mint mark on a Forest tile). Leaf is new. Mint is only 1.7:1 on white, too faint for the waves, so Leaf replaces it on light backgrounds. CMYK and Pantone values are starting points; proof them with the printer.

**Contrast:** Forest on White 12.4:1. Mint on Forest 7.2:1 (both fine for text). Leaf on White **3.4:1**: fine for the logo and large graphics, but **not for small body text**; use Forest. **Never use Mint on white.**

**Approved pairs:** colour (Forest + Leaf) on White · reversed (White + Mint) on Forest · Mint one-colour on Forest (app icon, like the OpenPiso favicon) · black on White. On photos, use white or black on a calm area.

## 5. Typography
- The wordmark is **drawn, not typed**. It's built from the symbol's circles and stroke, so there's no font licence to worry about. Always use the files; never retype "openpiso wifi" in a font.
- For UI and docs text, pair it with a geometric sans under an open licence (e.g. **Outfit** or **Figtree**, both OFL). Fall back to `system-ui`.

## 6. Don'ts
Don't stretch, squash, or rotate. Don't recolour outside the palette, and don't swap which parts are the light green. Don't add shadows, outlines, gradients, or glows. Don't rearrange or resize the parts of a lockup. Don't use the full symbol below 32 px (use the small cut). Don't place it on busy backgrounds without a solid tile.

## 7. Source
Masters are generated by `brand/logo/build_kit.py` (geometry: stroke 24, O radius 64, dot 20, waves 16 apart). Icons and variants come from the skill's `export_variants.py`. See `HANDOVER.md`.
